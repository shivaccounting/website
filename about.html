Our story

From one bookkeeper to a dedicated team.

We started in 2019 as a small bookkeeping practice in Gujarat, India, founded by Kailash Dabhi. Today we're a growing team and a trusted outsourcing partner for US CPAs, small businesses, law firms and e-commerce companies.

We bring 5+ years of hands-on accounting experience and strict attention to US standards — so our clients get clarity in their numbers and can focus on growth.
